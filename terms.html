Premium subscription

  • Premium costs £2.99 per month and removes adverts, with a higher AI allowance and extra features as listed on the Premium screen.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID, Google Play account or card when you confirm the purchase.
  • It renews automatically each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancel any time in your store’s subscription settings. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period; no further charges are made.

  • If Premium ends, your account returns to the Free plan and your saved data is kept.
  • Subscriptions are linked to your DAYMATE account. Sign in on a new device and tap Restore purchases.

Adverts

Free accounts may show adverts if you allow ad cookies. See the privacy policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings. This permanently removes your data. An active store subscription is not cancelled automatically — cancel it in your store settings.
